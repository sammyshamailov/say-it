# Pictures for the 28 new words: 4 ChatGPT runs

For each run: open a **new** ChatGPT chat, attach the reference sheet named for that run, paste the prompt, and save the result under the name given (as .webp or .png). Then send me the 4 images.

Reference sheets: `assets/pictures/sheets/actions-boy.webp` for the boy runs, `actions-girl.webp` for the girl runs (sent to Sammy as PNG on 2026-11-23). Save the results in `assets/pictures/sheets/`.

Written 2026-11-23 to replace the first prompts in `docs/NEW-WORDS.md`, which described only the boy (the girl runs need her own hair and clothes) and asked for realistic proportions while the reference sheets are rounder cartoon children.

**Before saving, check:**
- exactly 16 (or 12) separate pictures, in the listed order;
- the same child in every cell, looking like the reference (hair, clothes, face);
- no text or letters anywhere (the stop sign is a blank red octagon);
- nothing touches its neighbour.

**Harder pictures, compare them side by side:** HELLO must not look like the existing *wave* picture, and BYE must clearly be leaving. GIVE, MORE and PLEASE are all "hands toward someone". STOP and NO are both stern: the octagon and the X must be big. If one can't be told apart from another, regenerate just that sheet or we drop the word.

## Run 1: Actions 2, BOY

Attach: **actions-boy.png** · Save as: **actions2-boy.webp**

```
Create ONE image: a sprite sheet of exactly 16 separate illustrations for a preschool vocabulary app, arranged in an invisible 4-column × 4-row grid of equal square cells.

REFERENCE: the attached image is the style and character reference. Every cell shows the SAME child as in the reference: a 5-year-old BOY with short, tousled brown hair, light tan skin, a plain mint-green T-shirt, blue shorts and blue sneakers with white soles. Same face, hair, clothes, body proportions and drawing style as the reference, in every cell.

ITEMS, left to right, top to bottom (exactly this order, exactly these items):
1. JUMP: jumping high, both feet off the ground, knees bent, arms up
2. SIT: sitting on a small wooden chair, hands on knees (chair in side profile)
3. STAND: standing still and straight, arms at the sides
4. PLAY: kneeling on the floor and stacking three colored wooden blocks
5. GIVE: holding out a red ball with both hands, arms stretched forward
6. HUG: hugging a brown teddy bear tightly with both arms
7. KISS: blowing a kiss from an open hand, one small red heart floating beside the hand
8. WASH: washing hands under a small tap, soap bubbles on the hands
9. LOOK: looking through a magnifying glass held up to one eye
10. LISTEN: one hand cupped behind the ear, head tilted
11. THROW: throwing a blue ball overhand, the ball just leaving the hand
12. CATCH: catching a blue ball with both hands in front of the chest
13. PUSH: pushing a large cardboard box with both hands, leaning forward
14. PULL: pulling a small red wagon by its handle, leaning back
15. BRUSH: brushing teeth with a toothbrush, mouth open, a little foam
16. HIDE: crouching behind a large cardboard box, only the head and hands peeking over the top

POSE: full body, in a 3/4 view turned toward the LEFT, doing the action described, with only the props and marks listed. The gesture and the facial expression must be strong and unmistakable. Camera at the child's chest height.

STYLE (copy the reference exactly, do not vary or reinterpret):
- The same children's picture-book cartoon style as the reference: the same head-to-body proportions, the same face and eyes, the same line weight. Do not make the child more realistic, older, or more chibi than the reference.
- Clean cel shading as in the reference: one base color, one shadow tone and one highlight per area, light from the top-left. No gradients, no textures.
- Natural, slightly bright colors, the same palette as the reference.
- The same even dark outline as the reference around every illustration.
- NO sticker border, NO white outline around the illustration.

SIZE AND PLACEMENT:
- Scale every item so its widest dimension fills 80% of its cell, props and marks included.
- Center each item in its cell. Items that stand share the same invisible ground line near the bottom of the cell.

BACKGROUND AND FORBIDDEN:
- Flat solid pure white (#FFFFFF) background over the whole image.
- Do NOT draw the grid: no boxes, frames, cell borders, cards, lines or dividers.
- No ground, grass, cast shadows, scenery, extra props, accessories or clothing unless listed for that item.
- NO text, letters, words, numbers, labels or watermarks anywhere. Signs and marks are blank shapes.
- Do not add anything that is not described. Nothing may touch or overlap a neighbouring cell.
```
